# 2026 Objectives

## Overview

2026 is focused on building a strong foundation in quantitative finance, statistical modeling, and low-latency trading systems engineering.  

The objective is not merely to implement strategies, but to design and understand the full stack of a trading infrastructure — from mathematical modeling to execution systems and performance optimization.

---

## Core Pillars

### 1. Mathematical & Statistical Foundations

Strengthen applied understanding of:

- Linear algebra 
- Probability theory and stochastic processes
- Statistical inference and estimation theory
- Time series analysis and validation frameworks
- Volatility modeling and regime detection
- Markov Chains and Monte Carlo Simulations

**Deliverables**
- Research notes on time series validation
- Volatility estimation study
- Regime detection experiment
- Documented statistical modeling experiments

---

### 2. Market Microstructure & Execution Modeling

Develop working knowledge of:

- Limit order book dynamics
- Spread formation and price impact
- Queue position and fill probability
- Slippage modeling
- Transaction cost analysis
- Inventory risk management

**Deliverables**
- Order book dynamics research report
- Execution cost modeling study
- Empirical slippage analysis
- Microstructure experiment logs

---

### 3. Low-Latency Systems Engineering (C++ Focus)

Design and benchmark performance-oriented systems with emphasis on:

- Memory layout and cache locality
- RAII and move semantics
- Lock-free data structures
- Event-driven architectures
- Profiling and latency measurement
- Serialization performance

**Deliverables**
- Event-driven engine design documentation
- Latency benchmark reports (microsecond/nanosecond level)
- Memory optimization analysis
- Lock-free structure implementation notes

---

### 4. Modeling & Research Infrastructure (Python Focus)

Build a structured research workflow including:

- Feature engineering pipelines
- Rolling-window and walk-forward validation
- Model performance evaluation frameworks
- Risk-adjusted metric computation
- Backtesting architecture design

**Deliverables**
- Backtesting system architecture documentation
- Statistical modeling experiments
- Model inference benchmark reports
- Validation methodology documentation

---

### 5. Risk & System Stability

Design risk controls and monitoring layers:

- Position and exposure limits
- Drawdown controls
- Kill-switch mechanisms
- Monitoring dashboards
- Logging and diagnostics systems

**Deliverables**
- Risk management architecture document
- Monitoring system design
- Stability and failure-case analysis
